About, privacy and support

Karigar is a ledger for jewellery shops. It keeps your suppliers, customers, gold and silver weights, balances, notes and photos in your own Google Sheet and Google Drive.

Support

Something not working, a question, or an idea? Write to us and we will reply as soon as we can.

Email support

When you write, tell us your shop name, what you were doing, what you expected and what happened. A screenshot helps. Never send your shop PIN or your license key. We will never ask for them.

Where the rates come from

  • The world price. Gold, silver and platinum prices come from gold-api.com, checked against a second source (Coinbase). The dollar-to-rupee rate comes from open.er-api.com. If the two price sources differ by more than 3%, the app says so.
  • The sum. World price per ounce, times the dollar rate, divided by 31.1035 grams in a troy ounce, times the purity (22K is 22 parts in 24, 925 silver is 92.5%).
  • India’s duty. A shop in India pays import duty on bullion, so the app adds a percentage on top. It starts at 6% for gold and silver and 6.4% for platinum, which were India’s duty rates when this was written. Duty changes, so check it.
  • Match your dealer. On the Today page, open “Match my dealer’s rate”, type what your dealer quoted (per gram, per 10 grams or per kilogram) and the app adjusts itself to match. A rate you type on the Metals page always wins.

Forgot your PIN?

Your PIN is stored in the script that runs your Sheet. Open your Google Sheet, choose Extensions, then Apps Script, and look at the top of the code for SCRIPT_PIN. To change it, edit that line, save, then choose Deploy, Manage deployments, the pencil, New version, Deploy. The new PIN works straight away.

After 8 wrong tries the app locks for 10 minutes. That protects your books from guessing.

Backups

Your books already live in your Google Sheet. For a copy of your own, open Sheet in the app and tap Download a backup. It saves one file with your suppliers, customers and entries. Photos stay in your Google Drive folder.

Privacy

Karigar has no accounts, no adverts and no tracking. It does not collect your shop’s data. Here is where things go.

  • In your Google Sheet: suppliers, customers, entries, notes, categories and the demo data if you load it. Customer balances are also written to a readable tab for your accountant.
  • In your Google Drive: the photos you take on a sale or return, in a folder called “Karigar photos”. The script only reads and changes files in that folder.
  • On this device: the link to your Sheet, a copy of your books so the app opens fast, your license key, your text size and the last live rates. The PIN is kept only while the tab is open. Clear the browser’s site data to remove the device copy.
  • Sent to other services: the app asks gold-api.com, coinbase.com and open.er-api.com for prices. No shop or customer information goes with those requests, but those services can see your device’s internet address. Fonts come from Fontshare and Google Fonts, which can see it too. Your Sheet is read and written through Google Apps Script.
  • Your license: the app checks your license key with the license service so it can tell whether your plan is active.
