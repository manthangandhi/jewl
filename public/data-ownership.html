DATA OWNERSHIP

Your ledger. Your Google Drive.

Your supplier ledger, transactions, reports, attachments and backups are designed to live in your Google account.

Karigar keeps only the tenant, account, plan, entitlement and billing metadata required to operate the software. We do not put supplier names, balances, transaction amounts or invoices into the SaaS control-plane database.

If you stop paying, the application can become read-only, but your Google files are not deleted. You can export your information and retain control of the underlying Drive data.
