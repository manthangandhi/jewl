About, privacy and support

Karigar is a ledger for jewellery shops. It keeps your suppliers, customers, gold and silver weights, balances, notes and photos in your own Google Sheet and Google Drive.

Support

Something not working, a question, or an idea? Write to us and we will reply as soon as we can.

Email support

When you write, tell us your shop name, what you were doing, what you expected and what happened. A screenshot helps. Never send your shop PIN or your license key. We will never ask for them.

Privacy

Karigar has no accounts, no adverts and no tracking. It does not collect your shop’s data. Here is where things go.

  • In your Google Sheet: suppliers, customers, entries, notes, categories and the demo data if you load it. Customer balances are also written to a readable tab for your accountant.
  • In your Google Drive: the photos you take on a sale or return, in a folder called “Karigar photos”. The script only reads and changes files in that folder.
  • On this device: the link to your Sheet, a copy of your books so the app opens fast, your license key, your text size and the last live rates. The PIN is kept only while the tab is open. Clear the browser’s site data to remove the device copy.
  • Sent to other services: the app asks gold-api.com and open.er-api.com for prices. No shop or customer information goes with those requests, but those services can see your device’s internet address. Fonts come from Fontshare and Google Fonts, which can see it too. Your Sheet is read and written through Google Apps Script.
  • Your license: the app checks your license key with the license service so it can tell whether your plan is active.
